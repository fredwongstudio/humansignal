# Human Signal

**Palantir Deployment Strategist Proof of Work**

> **The computer knows what was recorded. The humans know what actually happened.**

Human Signal is a prototype exploring a simple idea:

**What if the operational knowledge, frustrations, observations, and suggestions of employees could become structured enterprise data?**

## The Missing Data Layer

Enterprise systems capture transactions, workflows, finance, operations, supply chain, CRM, sensors, and other machine-generated data.

But much of what is actually happening on the ground never becomes data.

Employees experience:

* repetitive manual work
* inefficient processes
* unnecessary approvals
* workarounds
* missing information
* recurring operational friction
* customer pain
* ideas for improvement

Most of these signals disappear.

People complain to colleagues, work around the problem, and carry on.

The organisation never sees the signal.

## Human Signal

Human Signal gives employees two simple ways to contribute:

**Something isn't working**
Report friction, pain, inefficiency, or a workaround.

**I have an idea**
Suggest an improvement or opportunity.

Employees speak or type naturally.

AI does the heavy lifting:

**Capture → Structure → Connect → Synthesise**

For example:

> “Every Monday I spend almost two hours matching supplier invoices against purchase orders because their descriptions never match ours.”

can become:

**Function:** Finance Operations
**Process:** Invoice reconciliation
**Friction:** Manual matching
**Frequency:** Weekly
**Reported effort:** ~2 hours
**Possible dependency:** Supplier product data
**Evidence:** Employee observation
**Status:** Unvalidated

> **Human input is evidence, not automatically truth.**

Human Signal preserves the original source, connects related employee observations, and tests them against enterprise system data.

## The MVP

The prototype demonstrates one operational loop:

**CAPTURE**
Employee describes the pain.

↓

**STRUCTURE**
AI turns unstructured human input into operational evidence.

↓

**PATTERN**
Related signals reveal recurring friction.

↓

**SYNTHESISE**
Human evidence is connected with system data.

↓

**ACTION**
A better operational question and investigation emerge.

↓

**MEASURE**
Did the intervention actually make the work better?

## The Core Idea

One complaint is an anecdote.

Thousands of structured employee observations can become a new enterprise data layer.

Human Signal connects:

**What people experience**

with

**What enterprise systems record**

to build a more complete picture of how work is actually happening.

## Why I Built This

I built Human Signal as a proof of work for a Palantir Deployment Strategist application.

It reflects how I approach problems:

**What are we trying to solve?**
**Is this even a problem?**
**Is this actually the problem?**

I believe the job is not to start with the technology or the dashboard.

It is to get close to reality, connect fragmented evidence, find the question that matters, and turn that understanding into action.
