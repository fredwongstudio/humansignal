WHY THIS MATTERS

The pain usually disappears before it becomes data.

People complain to colleagues, work around the problem, and keep moving. Human Signal creates a low-friction way for that experience to enter the organisation’s data layer.

AI takes on the burden of listening, clarifying and structuring. Humans keep doing the work that matters.
